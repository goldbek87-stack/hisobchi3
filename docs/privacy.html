Ilova haqida

Hisobchi — ovoz yoki qo'lda kiritish orqali kundalik xarajatlarni yozib boradigan va hisobot chiqaradigan Android ilovasi. Ilovani Farrux Sadridinov (keyingi o'rinlarda "dasturchi") yaratgan.

Qisqacha

Ilova sizdan ro'yxatdan o'tishni so'ramaydi, sizning ma'lumotlaringizni dasturchining serveriga yubormaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga sotmaydi. Siz kiritgan xarajatlar faqat o'z telefoningizda saqlanadi.

Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanadi

  • Siz kiritgan xarajat yozuvlari (summa, izoh, sana, toifa) va ilova sozlamalari.
  • Bu ma'lumotlar faqat qurilmangizdagi ilova xotirasida turadi. Ilovani o'chirsangiz, ular ham o'chadi.

Mikrofon va ovozli kiritish

Xarajatni ovoz bilan aytish uchun ilova mikrofon ruxsatini so'raydi. Ovozni matnga aylantirishni Android tizimining nutqni tanish xizmati (odatda Google xizmati) bajaradi. Ilovaning o'zi ovozni yozib olmaydi va saqlamaydi, u faqat tizimdan qaytgan matnni oladi. Tizim xizmati ovozni qanday qayta ishlashi qurilmangiz sozlamalariga va Google'ning maxfiylik siyosatiga bog'liq. Ovozli kiritishdan foydalanmasangiz, mikrofon ruxsatini bermasligingiz mumkin, ilova qo'lda kiritish bilan ishlayveradi.

Zahira nusxa va PDF hisobot

Zahira nusxa va PDF hisobotni siz o'zingiz so'raganingizda ilova fayl sifatida yaratadi. Faylni qayerga saqlash yoki kimga yuborishni siz Android'ning ulashish oynasida o'zingiz tanlaysiz. Faylni tanlagan ilovangizga (Telegram, Google Drive va boshqalar) ulashganingizdan keyin u shu xizmatning qoidalariga bo'ysunadi. Zahira nusxadan tiklashda siz tanlagan fayl ilovaga o'qiladi.

Internet

Ilova o'z serverlariga ma'lumot yubormaydi. Ilova ko'rinishi uchun Google Fonts shriftini yuklashi mumkin, bunda Google'ga sizning IP manzilingiz va qurilma haqidagi oddiy texnik ma'lumot yetib boradi. Shrift yuklanmasa, ilova tizim shriftidan foydalanadi.

Reklama va kuzatuv

Ilovada reklama, tahlil (analytics) va kuzatuv vositalari yo'q.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalar uchun mo'ljallanmagan va ularning ma'lumotlarini ongli ravishda yig'maydi.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Ma'lumotlaringiz sizning telefoningizda bo'lgani uchun ularni ilova ichidan o'chirishingiz yoki ilovani o'chirib tashlashingiz mumkin. Dasturchi sizning ma'lumotlaringizga kira olmaydi.

About the app

Hisobchi is an Android app for tracking daily expenses by voice or manual entry and producing reports. It is developed by Farrux Sadridinov ("the developer").

Summary

The app does not require an account, does not send your data to the developer's servers, and does not sell data to third parties. The expenses you enter are stored only on your own phone.

What data is stored

  • The expense records you enter (amount, note, date, category) and app settings.
  • This data stays in the app's storage on your device. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted.

Microphone and voice input

To let you speak an expense, the app asks for microphone permission. Speech is converted to text by the Android system speech recognition service (usually provided by Google). The app itself does not record or store audio; it only receives the resulting text. How the system service processes speech depends on your device settings and Google's privacy policy. If you do not use voice input, you can deny the microphone permission and the app will still work with manual entry.

Backup and PDF reports

The app creates a backup or PDF report file only when you ask for it. You choose where to save it or whom to send it to in Android's share dialog. Once you share a file to another app (Telegram, Google Drive, etc.), it is governed by that service's rules. When restoring from a backup, the file you select is read into the app.

Internet

The app does not send data to its own servers. For its appearance it may load the Google Fonts typeface, which means Google receives your IP address and basic technical request information. If the font cannot load, the app uses a system font.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your phone, you can delete it inside the app or by uninstalling the app. The developer cannot access your data.
